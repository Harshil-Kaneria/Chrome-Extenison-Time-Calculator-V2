Meet Time Calculator V2, the beautifully simple Chrome extension that helps you track your work hours and manage your time without the hassle.

Whether you're working a standard 9 to 5, doing a half-day, or managing flexible hours, Time Calculator is here to make your day completely predictable and stress-free.

🚀 What's New in Version 2!
⭐️ Beautiful Redesign: A gorgeous new look with smooth progress bars and 20 custom background colors.
⭐️ Quick Setup Presets: One click to set up a "9 to 5", "8 hours + break", or "Half Day" schedule.
⭐️ Start Work at Now: Just click one button when you sit at your desk to start tracking immediately.
⭐️ Leave Targets: Need to log off at exactly 5 PM? Set a "Leave by" time to see a secondary progress bar showing the time you have left.
⭐️ Compact & Detailed Modes: Choose how much information you see on your dashboard. 

⭐️ Core Features:
⭐️ Real-time Time Tracking: Keep a close eye on your progress to effectively manage your valuable time.
⭐️ Timely Alerts: Get a browser notification automatically when your work time is up.
⭐️ Water Reminder: Turn on the built-in reminder to help you stay hydrated throughout your workday.
⭐️ Customizable Time Limits: Easily log your working hours, add your break time, and you're good to go.
⭐️ Runs in the Background: Even if you close the extension, your timers keep running safely and accurately.

With Time Calculator, you'll never lose track of time again or forget to take your breaks. Download now and start managing your most valuable asset—your time!
